Medical NFC

A medical NFC that speaks for you when every second matters.

MyMedicalNFC.com connects a bracelet, tag, card, or keychain to a private medical profile that can be opened in seconds. It can carry critical conditions, allergies, medications and doses, devices, emergency contacts, and additional health details in a cleaner format than a traditional engraved tag.

What this medical NFC can carry

  • Name, blood type, age, weight, height, and organ donor status
  • Conditions, medical allergies, food allergies, medications, doses, and devices
  • Emergency contacts for family, caregivers, or parents
  • Doctor, clinic, insurance, location details, and optional full record link

How it works

A simple flow from setup to emergency access

  1. Create the medical profile directly or send a private activation link to the client.
  2. Complete the health information once and review the emergency page preview.
  3. The secure link becomes the medical page connected to the bracelet, keychain, or card.
  4. When the NFC is scanned, the profile opens with emergency details ready to read.

Ideal for

A strong fit for families, seniors, chronic conditions, and active lifestyles

  • Diabetes, epilepsy, heart conditions, severe allergies, and medication-dependent patients
  • Children, older adults, caregivers, and family members who need faster emergency context
  • Athletes, travelers, drivers, hikers, and anyone who wants critical information always attached
  • People who want something cleaner, more complete, and more useful than a basic engraved bracelet

Profile management

The profile can be reopened later if medical information changes

If you want online access reopened later for remote edits, that service may have an additional cost.

  • Useful when medications, doses, diagnoses, or emergency contacts change.
  • The online page can be updated first, even if the physical NFC piece is not in hand yet.
  • The physical NFC is only reprogrammed when the administrator has the bracelet, tag, or keychain.
