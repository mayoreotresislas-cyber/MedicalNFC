Medical form

This page explains the medical form that will be linked to the NFC.

This page is for the person who will complete the medical information before the NFC is programmed. You will add one person's emergency details, extra medical information, family contacts, and the language that should appear when the profile is scanned.

Complete Medical Form

What you will fill

  • Basic identity and blood type
  • Conditions, allergies, medications, and devices
  • Emergency contacts for family or caregivers
  • Optional link to the complete medical record

How it works

A simple flow before the NFC is recorded

  1. Complete the medical profile form.
  2. Save the profile and generate the secure URL.
  3. The administrator receives that URL by email.
  4. That URL is the one recorded in the NFC tag.

Important

The medical data is not written directly to the NFC chip

The chip stores only a secure web address. That keeps the information easier to update and better organized for future changes.
