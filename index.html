How it works

A simple flow from setup to emergency access

  1. Create the medical profile directly or receive a private activation link from us.
  2. Complete the health information once and review the emergency page preview.
  3. The secure link becomes the medical page connected to the NFC tag.
  4. When the NFC is scanned, the profile opens with emergency details ready to read.

Ideal for

A strong fit for families, seniors, chronic conditions, and active lifestyles

  • Diabetes, epilepsy, heart conditions, severe allergies, and medication-dependent patients
  • Children, older adults, caregivers, and family members who need faster emergency context
  • Athletes, travelers, drivers, hikers, and anyone who wants critical information always attached
